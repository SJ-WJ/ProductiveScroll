# ProductiveScroll

Turn doomscrolling into a knowledge library. Save posts, videos and articles from any app on your phone or across the web, then sort and connect them on desktop.

- **Mobile (Inbox):** share from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X or the web and lightly tag
- **Desktop (Workshop):** browse a feed, group saves into boards, and connect ideas on a canvas
- **Browser extension:** save the page you're on in one click
